Program Overview

What is Equipment Financing?

Equipment financing is structured debt where the equipment being purchased serves as collateral. Because the lender holds a security interest in a tangible asset, rates are lower and approval is easier than unsecured options. Section 179 typically allows immediate tax deduction of the full purchase.

Ideal Borrower

Who This Program Is For

US businesses purchasing or refinancing business-use equipment: commercial vehicles, manufacturing machinery, medical/dental equipment, restaurant equipment, technology, construction equipment, or specialty assets.
