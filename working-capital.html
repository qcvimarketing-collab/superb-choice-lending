Program Overview

What is Working Capital?

Working capital loans give your business immediate access to unsecured funds based on monthly revenue and bank deposits — not collateral. We structure repayment around your cashflow pattern so payments never strain operations. Most clients receive a decision within 24 hours and funds within 48 hours of accepted term sheet.

Unlike traditional bank loans that can take 30-60 days and require extensive documentation, working capital programs are streamlined for speed. The trade-off is cost: rates run higher than SBA or bank term loans, but the speed and minimal documentation make them invaluable for time-sensitive needs.

Ideal Borrower

Who This Program Is For

Established US businesses with 6+ months of operating history and $15K+ in monthly revenue, who need fast access to capital for payroll, inventory, opportunistic deployment, marketing, or emergency repairs.
